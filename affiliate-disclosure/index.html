Affiliate Disclosure

How RealSkillSet uses affiliate links while maintaining transparent, independent course and platform comparisons.

Effective date: September 26, 2026.

Plain-language disclosure: Some links on RealSkillSet are affiliate links. If a visitor registers or purchases through one of those links, RealSkillSet may earn a commission. The visitor's price is not increased because of the commission.

Why affiliate links are used

Affiliate revenue can support research, writing, hosting and maintenance while keeping learning guides freely accessible. RealSkillSet does not charge learners to read comparison content.

How attribution works

An affiliate network or provider may use a referral identifier, cookie or similar technology after an outbound link is clicked. If a qualifying action occurs during the applicable tracking period, the provider or network may attribute the action to RealSkillSet.

Editorial independence

Affiliate availability should not guarantee a positive review, prominent ranking or omission of meaningful disadvantages. Comparisons should distinguish free access from paid credentials, identify important limitations and encourage visitors to verify the current provider terms.

Provider names and trademarks

Provider names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Reference to a provider does not imply sponsorship, endorsement or ownership unless explicitly stated.

Prices, discounts and availability

Providers control prices, taxes, promotions, course availability, certificate charges, subscriptions and refund rules. RealSkillSet cannot guarantee that a price or offer remains available. The provider's official page and checkout are authoritative at the time of transaction.

Sponsored content

If RealSkillSet publishes paid placement or sponsored content, that relationship should be labeled clearly so readers can distinguish advertising from independent editorial material.

Current affiliate-marketing expectations

Modern learners increasingly expect clear disclosure near recommendations, honest explanations of total certificate cost, transparent distinctions between free access and paid credentials, and useful comparisons that do more than repeat a provider's marketing copy.

Reader responsibility

Before registering or paying, review the provider's syllabus, assessment, cancellation, refund, certificate and privacy terms. For regulated or employer-required credentials, confirm recognition directly with the relevant authority or employer.
