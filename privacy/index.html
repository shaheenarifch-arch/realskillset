Privacy Policy

How RealSkillSet handles newsletter sign-ups, contact messages, affiliate link cookies, fonts, hosting logs and your privacy choices.

Effective date: September 29, 2026.

RealSkillSet is an independent learning guide. We collect as little personal information as possible and we do not sell personal information.

Who we are

RealSkillSet (realskillset.com) is based in Boston, Massachusetts, USA. Questions about this policy can be sent to agents@getservices.ai or +1 (313) 729-1534.

Information you give us

Newsletter. If you subscribe, we receive the email address you enter and your consent to receive emails. Sign-ups are delivered to us through Web3Forms, a form-forwarding service. We use the address only to send course guides and learning updates, and you can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any email or writing to us.

Messages. If you email or call us, we keep the details you share so we can reply.

Information collected automatically

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and pages requested for security and reliability. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the font files are requested.

Affiliate links and cookies

Some links to course providers go through the Awin affiliate network. When you click one, Awin and the provider may set cookies to record that the visit came from RealSkillSet so that a qualifying enrolment or purchase can be credited. Those cookies are controlled by Awin and the provider under their own privacy policies. RealSkillSet does not receive your name or payment details.

How we use information

  • To send the newsletter you asked for.
  • To answer questions and correction requests.
  • To keep the website secure and working.
  • To understand which guides are useful, using aggregate information only.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with the service providers named above, or when the law requires it.

Retention

Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Messages are kept only as long as needed to handle the request.

Your choices and rights

You can unsubscribe, ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it by writing to agents@getservices.ai. Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Economic Area), you may have additional rights, and we will honour them. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Children

RealSkillSet is intended for adult learners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when our tools or practices change. The effective date above shows the latest version.
