Privacy Policy

How RealSkillSet handles browsing data, email inquiries, newsletter preferences and affiliate links.

Last updated: September 26, 2026.

Information you provide

RealSkillSet may receive information you send by email or phone. Newsletter functionality may save an email preference in the visitor's browser unless a separate email service is configured.

Browser and analytics data

Hosting, security and analytics providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested URL, referral source and timestamps. This data supports security, performance and aggregate reporting.

Affiliate links

Outbound affiliate links may use identifiers or cookies so a partner can attribute a registration or purchase. Affiliate tracking does not increase the learner's price.

Data sharing

RealSkillSet does not sell personal information. Data may be processed by hosting, security, analytics, email and affiliate-service providers as needed to operate the site.

Your choices

You can block cookies through browser settings, avoid submitting contact information, unsubscribe from email communications, or request deletion of information directly controlled by RealSkillSet.
