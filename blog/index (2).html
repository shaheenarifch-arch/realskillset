Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about free online courses, certificates, language learning, course comparisons and RealSkillSet editorial practices.

Are online courses really free?
Some providers allow free access to lessons and assessments while charging for certificates, printed diplomas, graded projects or premium tools. Check the current course page before enrolling.
Can an online certificate help me get a job?
A relevant certificate can support an application by showing current learning and initiative. It works best with practical evidence, experience and a targeted CV.
Can I learn any language online?
Online language courses cover many widely spoken and regional languages. Availability, speaking practice and feedback differ by platform.
How does RealSkillSet choose courses?
RealSkillSet considers course scope, total cost, assessment, flexibility, credential transparency and practical career relevance.
Does RealSkillSet sell courses?
No. RealSkillSet publishes independent guides and sends learners to external providers. Some outbound links are affiliate links.
Are course prices guaranteed?
No. Providers control pricing, availability, discounts and certificate fees. Confirm all details on the provider’s official checkout page.
How do I report incorrect information?
Email agents@getservices.ai with the page URL, the disputed statement and a reliable source for the correction.
