Quick answers

Before you start learning.

Are online courses really free?
Many platforms provide free access to course lessons and assessments. Certificates, printed diplomas, graded projects or premium features may cost extra, so always check the provider's current terms before enrolling.
Can online certificates help with a job search?
A relevant certificate can support a job application by showing initiative, recent learning and job-specific knowledge. It works best when paired with practical projects, experience and a targeted CV.
Can I learn any language online?
Yes. Modern language courses cover widely spoken and regional languages through structured lessons, listening practice, vocabulary review and AI-guided conversation. Availability varies by provider.
How should I choose a course?
Start with a target job or practical outcome, compare course scope, estimated study time, assessment method, certificate cost and provider credibility, then choose the shortest course that closes the most important skill gap.
Does RealSkillSet sell courses?
No. RealSkillSet is an independent learning guide. Some outbound links are affiliate links, which may earn a commission at no extra cost to the learner.
