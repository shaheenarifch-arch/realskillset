Quick answers

Before you start learning.

Are online courses really free?
Many platforms provide free access to lessons. Certificates, graded projects and premium features may cost extra, so confirm current provider terms.
Can online certificates help with a job search?
A relevant certificate can support an application when paired with practical projects, experience and a targeted CV.
Can I learn languages online?
Online language courses can provide structured lessons, listening practice, vocabulary review and conversation practice. Availability varies by provider.
Does RealSkillSet sell courses?
No. RealSkillSet is an independent learning guide. Some outbound links are affiliate links.
