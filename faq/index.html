Many learning platforms offer free course access while charging separately for certificates.

Can certificates help with job applications?

Certificates can strengthen a CV when combined with projects and practical experience.

Can I learn a language online?

Yes. Many modern platforms offer structured courses and speaking practice for dozens of languages.
