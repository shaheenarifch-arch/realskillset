Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed answers about free online courses, certificates, microcredentials, AI learning, language courses and RealSkillSet editorial practices.

These answers explain common course, certificate and language-learning questions. Provider terms can change, so verify final details on the official provider page.

Are online courses really free?
Some providers offer full lesson access without tuition but charge for certificates, printed diplomas, identity checks, graded projects or premium services. “Free course” and “free credential” are not always the same.
Can an online certificate help with employment?
A relevant certificate can demonstrate recent learning and initiative. The strongest applications also include practical projects, role-specific language, experience and a targeted CV.
Can I learn any language online?
Many widely spoken and regional languages are available through structured lessons, vocabulary review, listening exercises and guided conversation. Provider coverage and feedback quality vary.
What is a microcredential?
A microcredential is a short, focused record of learning. Recognition varies by issuer, employer and profession, so check who issues the credential and whether the target employer or regulator accepts it.
How should I choose a course?
Begin with a defined outcome, compare syllabus depth, level, study time, assessment, total credential cost and flexibility, then choose the shortest credible option that closes the most important skill gap.
Are AI-powered courses reliable?
AI can provide explanations, practice and feedback, but AI output may be inaccurate. Important technical, legal, medical or regulated information should be checked against authoritative sources.
Does RealSkillSet sell courses?
No. RealSkillSet is an independent guide. Learners register and pay on external provider websites.
How does RealSkillSet earn money?
Some outbound links are affiliate links. A qualifying registration or purchase may generate a commission without increasing the learner’s price.
Are prices guaranteed?
No. Providers control pricing, discounts, taxes, availability, certificate fees and refund rules. Confirm the final terms before enrolling or paying.
How can I report incorrect information?
Email agents@getservices.ai with the URL, disputed statement and a reliable source supporting the correction.
